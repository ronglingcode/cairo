# Bid Step Up

* Entry:
  * Price has been going down but slowed down and maybe start consolidating.
  * Preferablely the pause of the drop happens near some tecnicial level.
  * But there is still some distance to the next large bid wall. 
  * Then a new wall appear at a higher price. Enter the long trade with market order when seeing the higher wall appears.
  * It is highly recommended for manual trading that the stepped-up bid is above the low of day. Automation requires it.

* Stop loss: 
  * low of the day


## Examples
2026-05-08 FLNC

I was initially trading the Gap and Go. After stopping out, it pullback to premarket high and vwap. The price action is choppy around premarket high and vwap. One candle above, next candle below. We don't blindly buy the dip when price pulls back to a support. As it's consolidating there, there was only large bid at 22. Then a new higher at 24 formed. That's when we enter the long position. And this one continue to form new higher bid at 25 after we enter the trade, suggesting strong reason to hold/add.
